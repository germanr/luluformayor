Meet the Candidate.

Lulu's story is the American story. Born in Tennessee, adopted in Connecticut, she found her forever home in Middlebury, where she has spent the last year spreading joy down College Street, summiting peaks she has no business summiting, and licking the faces of new friends with relentless enthusiasm.

Her name, fittingly, means "an outstanding example of a particular type of person or thing." Her platform is simple: more walks, fewer baths, chicken in every bowl.

Why Lulu

Qualifications, credentials, and conviction.

Avid hiker

Conquered Vermont's tallest peak despite standing less than one foot tall. Proof that size doesn't matter when grit does.

Excellent Sniffer

An olfactory talent unmatched in Addison County. If something is hidden, Lulu will find it.

Chicken Connoisseur

A discerning palate is the mark of a discerning leader.

Master of Diplomacy

Maintains excellent relations with humans and dogs alike.

Squirrel Reconnaissance

Extensive field experience tracking the town's most elusive constituents. Always vigilant.

Masterful Napper

Can nap anytime, anywhere, on any surface.
